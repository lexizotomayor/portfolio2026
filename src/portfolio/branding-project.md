## Overview

Solstice is a small-batch coffee roaster founded by two friends with a philosophy as carefully considered as their roast profiles: seasonal, relationship-driven, transparent. They came to me needing a brand identity that could hold that weight — something that felt warm but sophisticated, artisan but not precious.

## The Brief

The founders wanted to avoid the clichés of third-wave coffee branding: no minimalist sans-serifs, no vintage-revival stamps. They wanted something that felt genuinely their own. Grounded, a bit poetic, built to last.

## Process

I spent the first week just listening and researching — understanding the supply chain relationships they'd built, the sensory language they used to describe their coffees, the spaces where their product would live.

From there, I explored three distinct directions:

1. **Celestial** — Leaning into the name, using astronomical motifs and serif typography
2. **Organic** — Hand-drawn textures, earthy warmth, tactile feel
3. **Editorial** — Clean, typographic-led, confident

The founders responded to the celestial direction but wanted it grounded — less "outer space," more "changing of seasons."

## Final Identity

The final mark is a stylized sun/compass rose that doubles as a roast wheel — a quiet nod to the craft. The wordmark uses a custom-modified serif that balances old-world warmth with contemporary legibility.

**Deliverables included:**

- Primary and secondary logomarks
- Full color system (warm neutrals with a single accent)
- Typography pairing guidelines
- Packaging design for three SKUs (100g, 250g, 1kg)
- Brand guidelines document
- Digital asset kit

## Result

Solstice launched their rebrand at a regional coffee festival and sold out their first seasonal release within 48 hours. The packaging has since been featured in two design publications.
